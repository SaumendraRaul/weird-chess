# Weird Chess v3.5 — Modes

- Added 8 alternate modes plus Classic.
- King Hunt: physically capture the King; check rules are disabled.
- Horde: configurable horde side with a giant pawn swarm.
- Sudden Death: outer rings collapse inward.
- Evolution Race: first to configurable Evolution count wins.
- Draft Chess: point-budget custom armies.
- Boss Battle: one super-powered Boss King side.
- Infection: captured non-Kings convert and respawn on the attacker's origin square.
- Random Army: equal random material sets from the deterministic match seed.
- Modes serialize into save/match-code/network state and state hashes.
- Stockfish is restricted to orthodox Classic mode.
- Kings no longer participate in Evolution in any form.
